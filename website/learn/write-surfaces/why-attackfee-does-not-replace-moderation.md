# Why Attackfee does not replace moderation

A valid Attackfee pass means the write already paid its cost, in work or, for an automated client on a site that takes payment, in money. It does not mean the comment, message, or account request is acceptable. Review, holds, filters you already run, and removal remain your job. Attackfee does not read the text and does not classify it. Form contents never leave the page through Attackfee.

That limit is part of the design. The product prices the write so volume is no longer free. It does not try to decide what should be published. A funded sender can still pay the cost and post junk. Your moderation queue, hold-for-review setting, or abuse process still has to handle what gets through.

People never pay money. Their browser earns a work pass in the background. Reading stays free.

## Where Attackfee fits

Install Attackfee on the write, and keep the review path you already trust. See [Protecting a comment section](/learn/write-surfaces/comment-sections), [What Attackfee is not for](/learn/limits/what-attackfee-is-not-for), and [What a funded attacker can still do](/learn/limits/funded-attackers).
